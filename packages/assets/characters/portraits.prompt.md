# Character portrait specification

Use case: `stylized-concept`

Asset type: square game Character portrait for the AgentWolf Character-card library.

Art direction: polished contemporary Japanese animation illustration with clean linework and
nuanced cel shading. Use a centered head-and-shoulders composition with the face, hair, and hats
inside a circular-crop-safe area. Light the subject with cool moonlight and restrained warm rim
light against abstract ink-navy and graphite tribunal geometry. Render one recognizable Character
only, with no text, logo, watermark, frame, UI, extra props, photorealism, or chibi exaggeration.

The Edogawa Conan portrait is the style, lighting, palette, and composition reference for the other
eleven portraits. It is not an identity reference.

Subject set:

- 江户川柯南: round glasses, dark cowlick, blue suit, red bow tie, alert expression.
- 工藤新一: teenage detective, dark forward hair tuft, blue school blazer, confident expression.
- 毛利兰: long dark hair, distinctive pointed forelock, blue school uniform, gentle determination.
- 毛利小五郎: middle-aged detective, brushed-back hair, narrow moustache, blue-gray suit.
- 灰原哀: short ash-brown bob, pale teal-gray eyes, guarded expression, dark high collar.
- 阿笠博士: bald crown, fluffy gray side hair, round glasses, broad moustache, white lab coat.
- 服部平次: tan teenage detective, swept spiky forelock, dark school jacket, energetic confidence.
- 远山和叶: high dark ponytail, green-brown eyes, sailor uniform, warm direct expression.
- 怪盗基德: white top hat and suit, blue band, red tie, monocle, elegant playful composure.
- 赤井秀一: adult marksman, green eyes, dark knit cap and high-collar jacket, restrained intensity.
- 安室透: tan young adult, short blond hair, blue-gray eyes, beige jacket, controlled friendliness.
- 琴酒: adult man, long silver hair, green eyes, black fedora and coat, controlled menace.
