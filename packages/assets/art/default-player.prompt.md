# 默认玩家头像

使用内置 ImageGen 生成，输出人物头像用于未绑定 Character 的玩家。浏览器使用 256 × 256 的 WebP，圆形裁切由组件负责。

## 提示词

```text
Use case: illustration-story. One square DEFAULT PLAYER AVATAR for a Chinese folk-mystery werewolf game, 1024x1024. A neutral enigmatic human traveler, close-up head and shoulders, facing front with a calm watchful expression, no identifiable gender, simple dark hood framing a lightly engraved face. Fine hand-carved woodcut/linocut in old-paper ivory on charcoal black #181916, tiny restrained vermilion stitch on the hood. The portrait must be a striking readable face at only 48px display size. Large head centered, entire forehead/chin/hood contour inside the central 70 percent circle, enough background around edges for circular crop. Not a wolf, no animal, no crown, no role-specific weapon or magic object; this single portrait is shared by all players without a chosen character, irrespective of game role. No label, letters, numbers, symbols, border, watermark or UI. Dry ink, tactile printed grain, crisp meaningful cuts, not shiny, not photorealistic, not 3D.
```
